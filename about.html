About

Team

CQC is developed and maintained by the CQC Research Team. The paper describing CQC is currently under double-blind review. Once the review results are available, we will update this page with information about team members and acknowledgements of the people we wish to thank.

The current version is Release 1.0 (October 2026). Changes between releases are listed on the Corpus page.

How to cite
