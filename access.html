Data Access

Documentation is open to everyone. The annotated data are available to researchers for non-commercial research, after we approve an application and the researcher’s institution signs the data use agreement.

What is available, and to whom
TierContentsAccess
PublicThis website, user guide, datasheet, annotation scheme, processing code, aggregate statisticsOpen to everyone
ControlledTranscripts, TextGrids, speaker and session metadata, source timestamps and download scriptsApproved researchers under the Data Use Agreement
Not distributedChannel names, account handles, video titles, imagesNot shared with anyone

Who can apply

Researchers at universities and non-profit research institutions, in linguistics and in other fields such as sociology, gender studies, anthropology, psychology, and communication. Students apply with their supervisor as principal investigator.

We cannot approve projects that aim to infer identity from speech, identify speakers, clone or synthesize voices, or serve commercial purposes.

Agreement at a glance

  • Non-commercial research only, for the project described in the application.
  • No inference of gender identity, sexual orientation, or other identity attributes.
  • No attempt to identify or contact speakers, and no linking with other data.
  • No voice cloning, speech synthesis, or training of generative speech models.
  • No redistribution, including uploads to public repositories or AI services that keep data.
  • Secure storage; publish only aggregate results; no audio examples without permission.
  • Delete removed material within 30 days of our notice.
