Data Use Agreement

Chinese Queer Corpus (CQC) Data Use Agreement, version 1.0

This page reproduces the agreement for reading. The signed version is sent with an approved application. [TODO: remove this line if not needed]

This Agreement is between the CQC Data Custodian ([TODO: name, University at Buffalo]) (“Custodian”) and the Recipient named in the signed application, together with the Recipient’s institution (“Institution”).

1. Definitions

  1. Speaker: any person whose voice appears in a CQC source recording.
  2. Public Materials: documentation, datasheet, annotation schema, code, and aggregate statistics. These are not covered by this Agreement.
  3. Controlled Data: transcripts, TextGrids, speaker- and session-level metadata (including identity fields and self-labels), source identifiers, timestamps, download scripts, any audio obtained with them, and any acoustic measurements made at speaker, segment, or frame level.
  4. Derived Data: any data, annotations, models, or features created from Controlled Data.
  5. Authorized Users: the Recipient and the named team members listed in the application, each of whom has read this Agreement.

2. Permitted use

  1. Controlled Data may be used only for the non-commercial research project described in the application.
  2. Permitted purposes include phonetic, sociophonetic, linguistic, and social-scientific analysis, and the evaluation of speech technology performance across speaker groups in aggregate.
  3. A new project or a substantially changed purpose requires a new application.

3. Prohibited use

Authorized Users shall not:

  1. Infer, predict, or classify any Speaker’s gender identity, trans status, sex characteristics, sexual orientation, or other identity attribute from speech, text, or metadata, or train or evaluate any system for that purpose.
  2. Identify or attempt to identify any Speaker, including by searching for source recordings outside the provided scripts, linking Controlled Data with other datasets, or applying speaker recognition against external data.
  3. Contact any Speaker in connection with CQC.
  4. Use any Speaker’s voice for voice cloning, speech synthesis, voice conversion, or the training of generative speech models.
  5. Use Controlled Data for commercial purposes, surveillance, law enforcement, immigration control, content moderation targeting individuals, or any decision about an individual.
  6. Redistribute, sublicense, sell, or publish Controlled Data, or Derived Data that could reconstruct it, including by uploading to public repositories or model hubs, or by entering it into third-party AI services that retain data.
  7. Publish anything that discloses, or could reasonably reveal, a Speaker’s identity together with an identity attribute.

4. Security

  1. Controlled Data shall be stored on encrypted, access-controlled systems at the Institution and accessed only by Authorized Users.
  2. Recipient shall notify the Custodian within 72 hours of any suspected unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, and cooperate in remediation.

5. Publication

  1. Results may be published in aggregate. Group-level statistics shall not be reported for groups of fewer than [TODO: 3] speakers unless the Speakers cannot be distinguished.
  2. Audio clips, spectrograms of identifiable stretches, or verbatim quotations longer than [TODO: 20 characters] require prior written permission from the Custodian.
  3. Speakers shall be referred to only by CQC speaker IDs.
  4. Publications shall cite CQC as described on the Team & Citation page and state that the data were used under this Agreement.

6. Updates and removal

  1. The Custodian removes material when a Speaker requests it or when a source becomes unavailable, and issues updated releases.
  2. Within 30 days of a removal notice, Recipient shall delete the affected Controlled Data and Derived Data that contain it, and confirm deletion in writing. Aggregate results already published need not be withdrawn.
  3. Recipient shall use the most recent release for new analyses.

7. Term and termination

  1. This Agreement runs for [TODO: 3] years from approval and may be renewed.
  2. At the end of the term or the project, or upon breach, Recipient shall delete all Controlled Data and Derived Data and confirm in writing, except data that must be retained for research integrity, which remain under this Agreement.
  3. The Custodian may suspend access and notify the Recipient’s Institution of a breach.

8. General

  1. Controlled Data are provided as is, without warranty of accuracy or fitness for any purpose.
  2. Recipient is responsible for obtaining any ethics approval required by its Institution and for complying with the laws that apply to it.
  3. This Agreement does not transfer any copyright in source recordings, which remains with the rights holders.
